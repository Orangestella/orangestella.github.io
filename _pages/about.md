Hello, I'm **Guanyu Hou**. I am an MSc in Artificial Intelligence student at [The University of Manchester](https://www.manchester.ac.uk/). My research focuses on **AI safety and security**, with an emphasis on trustworthy large language models and multimodal systems.

I study the robustness of audio language models, safety evaluation of text-to-video models, backdoor vulnerabilities, and privacy in retrieval-augmented generation. My work has appeared at venues including CVPR, EMNLP, AAAI, ACM Multimedia Asia, ICASSP, and ICME.

I am interested in PhD opportunities in AI safety and security.
